The cast of The Long Shadow

People (ned, robert, catelyn, cersei, tyrion, bran) are built with MakeHuman /
MPFB2 (makehumancommunity.org) by scripts/cast/build_humans.py:
- MakeHuman base mesh, skins, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, teeth, system hair: CC0.
- "Medieval dress" by punkduck (Dress 03 pack): CC-BY 4.0.
- "f_dress_05" by Mindfront (Dress 03 pack): CC-BY 4.0.
- Hair from the Hair 02 pack by Elvaerwyn: CC-BY 4.0 (where used).
- Beards from the Bodyparts 05 pack: CC0.
- Menswear (tunics, doublets, trousers, boots, belts) is generated per body by
  scripts/cast/garments.py, textured with Poly Haven fabric scans (CC0).

Soldiers (paladin, guard, knight, heraklios and others) and every animation in
humanoid-anims.glb come from Adobe Mixamo (mixamo.com), used under Mixamo's
terms: free for use in games and other projects, not redistributed as assets.
