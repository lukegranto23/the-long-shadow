City stone scans (Poly Haven, CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license), fetched with
scripts/fetch-polyhaven.mjs: red_sandstone_wall, granite_wall, marble_01,
patterned_paving, clay_roof_tiles_02, white_stucco, castle_brick_02_red,
castle_wall_slates, thatch_roof_angled.

Qarth follows A Clash of Kings: three carved walls (red sandstone with beasts,
grey granite with war, black marble with revels), slender towers, jewelled
domes, the Temple of Memory and serpent fountains. Relief friezes are painted
procedurally by app/game3d/relief.ts.
Further Poly Haven CC0 scans: castle_wall_varriation (Red Keep), large_sandstone_blocks,
marble_tiles. King's Landing follows A Game of Thrones: the Red Keep of pale red stone
with seven drum towers and iron ramparts above the river mouth, the Great Sept of
Baelor with its dome and seven crystal towers, and the ruined Dragonpit.
Also from Poly Haven (CC0): white_sandstone_bricks, used for Highgarden and
the dressed stone of Casterly Rock. Crags are generated by app/game3d/crag.ts.
